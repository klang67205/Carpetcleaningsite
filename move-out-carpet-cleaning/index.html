Booking

What to put in your booking.

The right package

Count carpeted rooms, hallways and stairs. Choose standard ($99) or small ($75), or a pet package if pets lived there.

Access details

Unit number, gate code, parking notes, or lockbox and office key instructions go in the booking notes.

Who gets the invoice

Housecall Pro sends the invoice after the visit, and it can be paid online. Payment is due on receipt.

Questions first

Text (316) 232-8111 before booking if something about the unit is unusual.

Honest limits

What a cleaning can't change.

Wear and damage

Cleaning can't repair worn, burned, torn or faded carpet.

Old pet damage

Pet treatment helps, but deep or old urine may leave odor or stains. Permanent removal cannot be promised.

Your deposit

Deposit decisions belong to your landlord and your lease, not to the cleaning.

Landlords and managers

Booking several units? Each unit is its own booking. See the property managers page.
