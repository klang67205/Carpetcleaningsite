Terms of service

Last updated October 19, 2026

The plain-language terms for booking a cleaning with Wichita Carpet Cleaning Services.

Booking

  • Appointments are Monday through Friday. Same-day appointments are not offered; online booking opens two days ahead and shows only open times.
  • When you finish online booking, your appointment is scheduled and you get a text confirmation with the date and time.
  • No payment is needed to book. The online scheduler (Housecall Pro) checks your address for the service area before you choose a time.
  • Service area is approximate, about 15 miles from downtown Wichita. Your exact address decides whether it’s covered. On-base military housing is not serviced.

Prices

  • Published prices on this website apply, plus applicable tax. The standard package covers up to 5 rooms, 2 hallways and 1 staircase. A staircase counts as one area however many steps it has. Each additional room, hallway or staircase is $15.
  • Exceptionally large or unusual spaces, specialty fabrics and surfaces, and anything not listed are reviewed before service.

Payment

No payment or card is needed to book. Payment is due after the work: cash at the visit, or a payment link sent when the job is done, which can be paid by card, Apple Pay and similar methods. Checks are not accepted.

Preparation and access

Please make sure we can get in and reach the rooms being cleaned. Move fragile items off furniture and out of the way; standard couches and loveseats can be moved when practical. Add a note when you book if there is a gate, parking restriction or building instruction. See the preparation guide.

Changes and cancellations

There is no cancellation fee. Please give as much notice as you can. Reply to your scheduling text or text (316) 232-8111. A change or cancellation is final once we confirm it.

Results

Drying time and results depend on carpet construction, soil, staining, prior treatment, humidity and airflow. Permanent stain or odor removal cannot be promised.
