Carpet care guide

How often should you get your carpet cleaned?

For most homes, a professional cleaning about once a year keeps carpet looking its best. Homes with pets, young kids or heavy foot traffic often book every 6 months. In between, regular vacuuming and quick spot care do most of the work.

A simple guide

Match the schedule to your household.

Quiet homes

One or two adults, no pets, shoes off at the door: about once a year, or when traffic lanes start to show.

Pets

Shedding, paws and the occasional accident: about every 6 months, with pet treatment if there are spots.

Young kids

Spills, crumbs and floor play: about every 6 months for the rooms they use most.

Before or after big moments

Moving in or out, hosting the holidays, or listing a home are good times to schedule a cleaning.

These are general guidelines, not rules. Some carpet warranties ask for professional cleaning at set intervals, so check yours.

Signs it's time

You don't need a calendar to tell.

Traffic lanes

Darker paths in hallways and in front of the couch mean soil has built up in the fibers.

Matted areas

Pile that stays flat where people walk most.

Lingering smells

Pet or household odors that come back after vacuuming.

Spots that keep reappearing

A cleaned spot that slowly comes back usually means residue deeper down.

Between cleanings

Small habits that stretch the time between visits.

Vacuum often

Once or twice a week in busy rooms removes grit that wears carpet down.

Blot spills fast

Blot, don't rub, and use as little water as possible.

Use mats

A mat at each door catches a lot of dirt before it reaches the carpet.

Shoes off

The simplest way to keep traffic lanes from forming.
