The cleaning process

Modern low-moisture carpet cleaning.

Professional cleaning chemistry and counter-rotating agitation work together without heavily saturating the carpet.

  1. 01

    Pretreatment

    A professional cleaning solution is applied to loosen embedded soil.

  2. 02

    Counter-rotating agitation

    Professional brushes work through the carpet pile to mechanically loosen soil.

  3. 03

    Encapsulation cleaning

    Encapsulation chemistry surrounds loosened soil as the carpet dries, allowing it to be removed through subsequent vacuuming.

  4. 04

    Fast drying

    Because the process uses substantially less moisture than heavily saturated methods, carpets typically return to normal use faster.

This is professional low-moisture encapsulation cleaning technology. Wichita Carpet Cleaning Services does not claim CRI certification or endorsement.

Low-moisture cleaning is designed for fast drying. Actual time varies with humidity, airflow, carpet construction and soil conditions.

Small items such as couches and loveseats can be moved. Please move beds, large sectionals and other heavy furniture before arrival.
