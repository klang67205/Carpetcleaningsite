The helpful checklist

Five easy ways to make the visit smoother.

Do what is practical for your home. If something cannot be moved or needs special attention, point it out during the walkthrough.

  1. 01

    Clear loose items

    Pick up toys, clothing, cords, and small floor items where convenient.

  2. 02

    Protect breakables

    Move fragile items from furniture or paths that may need to be accessed.

  3. 03

    Plan furniture

    Standard couches and loveseats can be moved when practical. Move beds, large sectionals, and heavy pieces beforehand only if you want the carpet beneath them cleaned.

  4. 04

    Keep pets comfortable

    If a pet is nervous around equipment or an open door, a quiet room or crate can make the visit easier for them.

  5. 05

    Share priorities

    During the walkthrough, point out spots, traffic areas, pet concerns, or anything you want Keith to see.

Access and parking

No elaborate setup is needed.

A clear route helps

Make sure the rooms being cleaned can be reached safely. For apartments or controlled-access buildings, arrange entry with the office or provide the instructions in your booking notes.

Ordinary parking is enough

A driveway or legal street space near the service entrance is helpful. Add a note if there is a gate, alley, parking restriction, or building instruction Keith should know.

Plans changed?

Just send a simple request.

There is no cancellation fee. Give as much notice as possible by replying to your Housecall Pro text or texting (316) 232-8111. A change or cancellation is not final until Wichita Carpet Cleaning Services confirms it. Do not create a second online booking to reschedule an existing appointment.
