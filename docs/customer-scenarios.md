# Customer concierge scenarios

The booking guide handles these 25 customer paths using verified business information:

1. Standard $99 pricing
2. Package coverage
3. Additional-room pricing
4. Pet treatment
5. Drying time
6. Furniture
7. Preparation
8. Service area
9. On-base housing
10. Business hours
11. Booking availability
12. Same-day requests, with the next available future appointment shown instead
13. Cancellation
14. Rescheduling
15. Late arrival
16. Access instructions
17. Commercial or unusual work
18. Upholstery, tile, grout, or hard-floor questions
19. Stain expectations
20. Mold, sewage, flooding, or water damage
21. Payment
22. Invoice or receipt
23. A message sent through Housecall Pro
24. Complaints or damage concerns
25. Weather or chemical-sensitivity concerns

For a new booking, it sends the customer directly to the live Housecall Pro booking page. For an existing appointment, it sends the customer to the Manage Appointment link in that appointment's Housecall Pro confirmation. Messages sent by text through Housecall Pro are retained in its Customer Inbox and attached to the customer record. Housecall Pro Basic does not expose a public API or webhook that lets a separate website assistant identify, reschedule, cancel, edit, or automatically reply to an existing job. The confirmation link and the inbox are the available self-service boundary.
