Carpet care guide

How long does carpet take to dry after cleaning?

With our low-moisture process, carpet typically dries in about 1.5 to 2 hours under ordinary conditions. Heavily saturated methods put more water into the carpet, so their dry time depends much more on the equipment and the home.

Why it varies

Four things decide how fast carpet dries.

The same cleaning can dry faster in one room than another. These are the factors that matter most.

Humidity

Damp, muggy days slow drying. Dry winter air and running air conditioning speed it up.

Airflow

Moving air carries moisture away. A room with a running fan dries faster than a closed-off room.

Carpet construction

Thick, dense or long-pile carpet holds moisture longer than low, tight carpet.

Soil and spots

Heavily soiled traffic lanes and treated spots may get extra attention, so those areas can take a little longer.

Speed it up

Simple ways to help carpet dry faster.

Keep air moving

Set the thermostat fan to “on,” turn on ceiling fans, and point a box fan across the room if you have one.

Control humidity

In humid weather, air conditioning or a dehumidifier helps more than opening windows.

Walk carefully

Wait until the carpet feels dry to the touch. If you need to cross it sooner, wear clean socks or shoe covers instead of street shoes.

Pets and furniture

Keep pets off until the carpet is dry. Put furniture back once the carpet is dry so legs don’t sit on damp fibers.

Dry time is typical, not guaranteed. It varies with the carpet and the conditions in your home.
