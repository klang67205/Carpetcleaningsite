Choosing the right package

  • Count the carpeted rooms, hallways, and stairs in the unit.
  • Pick the package that covers all of them, then add any extra areas. The booking calculator does the math.
  • Use a pet-treatment package for units with pet issues.

Important limits

  • Results vary with the carpet and its condition.
  • Permanent stain or odor removal cannot be promised.
  • All prices are plus applicable tax.

How it works

Five steps, all online.

Each unit is its own booking, so every unit gets its own time slot, its own texts, and its own invoice.

  1. 01

    Book the unit

    Open the booking page, choose the package that fits the unit, and pick an open weekday time. Openings start two days out.

  2. 02

    Add access details

    Enter the unit address and number, plus lockbox or office key instructions, in the booking notes.

  3. 03

    Get the texts

    Housecall Pro texts a booking confirmation, then an on-the-way text, to the person who booked.

  4. 04

    Keith cleans

    Low-moisture cleaning with counter-rotating brushes, so the unit isn't left soaking wet.

  5. 05

    Pay the invoice

    The invoice arrives after the visit. Payment is due on receipt and can be made online.
