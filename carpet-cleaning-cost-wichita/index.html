With us, most homes pay $99 plus tax. That covers up to five rooms, two hallways and one standard staircase. The same area with pet treatment is $149, smaller visits start at $75, and each area beyond your package is $15. You see the price before you book and pay after the work is done.

What changes the price

Only three things move the total.

More rooms, halls or stairs

Each additional area, room, hallway or staircase beyond your package is $15, plus applicable tax. Add them in the booking calculator.

Pets

Pet spots or odor call for a pet-treatment package: $85 instead of $75, or $149 instead of $99.

Very large or unusual spaces

Exceptionally large or unusual spaces can be discussed before service, so nothing is a surprise on the day.

What doesn't change it

Closets stay included in their room. There is no payment to book and no cancellation fee.

Why one flat price

Pay for the clean, not the commercials.

No franchise overhead

No franchise royalties, billboards, TV ads or call center are built into the price.

Owner-operated

The owner does every job, so the price you see is for the cleaning itself.

No upsells

Published prices, no payment to book, and you pay after the work is done.

A low-moisture method

Counter-rotating brushes and low-moisture cleaning; carpet typically dries in about 1.5 to 2 hours.

Honest limits

What the carpet price doesn't cover.

Other services

Upholstery, tile and grout, sealed hard floors and commercial carpet are priced separately on their own pages.

Wear and damage

Cleaning can't repair worn, burned, torn or faded carpet.

Old pet damage

Pet treatment helps, but deep or old urine may leave odor or stains. Permanent stain or odor removal cannot be promised.

Scheduling

Appointments are Monday through Friday, online openings start two days out, and each home or unit is its own booking.
